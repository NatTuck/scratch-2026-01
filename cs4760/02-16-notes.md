## Today: How to Find a Job

Plan A: Already know someone who wants to hire you, optimally a family member
who's already the CEO of a big fully private company with not very good
auditing.

Plan B:

- Figure out what kind of job you want.
  - Actually qualified for.
  - Won't hate.
  - Location: Do you want to move to California? Alaska? Indonesia?
- Find / know people to give those documents to.
- How to find where to apply:
  - Know what company you want to work for, go to their website.
  - Plymouth State promotes Handshake
  - Nightmare trinity: LinkedIn, GlassDoor
  - Welcome to the Jungle - Might give better access to the other
    side of the thing for questions
  - Y-Combinator, Wellfound.com, Working Nomads
  - Especially in service industry, Talk to everyone you meet
    about work
- Generate a list of jobs to apply to
  - One interview per 20 applications
  - Don't get mad about 1 job offer per 30 applications
- Write Resume, Cover Letter
  - Schools publish resume templates.
  - There's no excuse not to have every resume and cover letter
    be 100% customized with chatbots existing.
  - Plymouth State career services will sit down with you and help.
  - Can get good photos done at career services, Silver Center
- Interview
  - Try not to have lied about your abilities
  - Try to be personable
  - Consider the STAR method; having this in your head will
    help give better interview question answers ; this also
    applies to cover letter stories
- Job Offer
  - Definitely don't just say yes immediately, sleep on it
  - Can reject
  - Accepting then turning down is sketchy, but sometimes correct
  - Try to avoid taking another job in exactly the same professional
    circle - dropping an iRobot offer for Boston Dynamics is obvious
